# Public-domain portrait fixture

`public_domain_portrait.jpg` is the official 2009 portrait of U.S. President Barack Obama,
photographed by Pete Souza as an official work of the United States Government. Source:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:President_Barack_Obama.jpg

The file is public domain in the United States (PD-USGov). It is used only by Android
instrumentation tests and is not packaged in production APK/AAB assets.
