# Milestone 6.2 Mode Calibration

Milestone 6.2 does not globally strengthen modes. It preserves the independently planned Milestone 6.1 envelopes and exposes their real accepted operations to the user.

For the exact sunrise regression, plans remain:

| Mode | Exposure | Shadows | Local midtone gamma delta | Face mean linear luminance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original | - | - | - | 0.09175 |
| Natural | +0.061 EV | +0.060 normalized | +0.132 | 0.10570 |
| Enhanced | +0.105 EV | +0.103 normalized | +0.183 | 0.11347 |
| Extreme | +0.148 EV | +0.146 normalized | +0.213 | 0.12012 |

All three were accepted at strength 1.0. Background mean luminance for Extreme changed only from 0.18472 to 0.18486; atmospheric protection therefore remains effective.

The real wedding modes could not be calibrated because their exact files are absent. It would be unsafe to increase group strength based only on the textual symptom. The adjustment panel now makes such future failures diagnosable: it will show whether face midtone correction was selected, reduced, rejected, or absent.
