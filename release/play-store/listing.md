## Full description

Restovue is a native, offline photo editor designed for private, non-destructive editing on your Android device.

Adjust exposure, color and detail; apply curated filters and LUTs; crop, rotate and correct perspective; use manual retouching, liquify, background masks, text, shapes, stickers and frames. Practical offline utilities include collages, batch conversion and compression, editable passport sheets, document and signature cleanup, multi-image exposure fusion and ordered panorama stitching.

Your selected photographs are processed locally. Restovue contains no advertising, analytics, cloud image processing or AI models. Files are opened with Android's system Photo Picker and saved through the Storage Access Framework.

Some advanced utilities have documented limits. Background removal is manual, passport layouts are editable guides rather than an acceptance guarantee, HDR uses exposure fusion, and panorama stitching works best with ordered horizontal overlap.

Developed by NextGen TechEdge Solutions.
